# Identity and permission decision

Use [session core](session-core.md) and, for its matching-grant/Leader/ambiguity conditions, [elevated authority](elevated-authority.md). Ambiguous account/actor identity needs the user's choice. Record `member` or `leader:<min(frozen ceiling, current permission)>` with delegated/task scope, approvals and unknowns; source/account/authorization/protection uncertainty stops dependent writes.

Grant/raise/restore/revoke persistent authority through `github-collaboration-setup` under existing default-branch rules. Daily does not activate proposed grants. A new session may retain a verified role only; task ownership still uses [claims/handoffs](claims-and-handoffs.md).
