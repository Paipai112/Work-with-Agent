# Verify elevated authority

Load when an active delegation matches the account/actor, a Leader action is requested, or identity/authority is ambiguous. [Session core](session-core.md) still applies. Read-only checks need no Leader calculation; mark only authority-dependent conclusions unknown when evidence is missing.

- Persistent Leader requires **exactly one** active project delegation at the verified default SHA, with independently verified human approval and completed governance activation. Match host/repository/default branch, stable account ID/login and responsibility `actor_id`; runtime/device/session are not grant keys. Validate the complete record using the caller's bundled Draft 2020-12 authorization schema and `FormatChecker()` with working `uri`/`date-time` checkers. `default_branch_source_sha` records approved proposal provenance, not today's HEAD. Local/proposed/PR grants, `granted_by`, revoked/superseded history and malformed/conflicting/unverifiable records cannot elevate.
- Effective write authority is frozen `permission_ceiling` ∩ fresh account rights ∩ delegated responsibility/**scope** ∩ project/inherited protection ∩ specific-action approval. Permission order: `read < triage < write < maintain < admin`. Maintain/Admin without a valid grant is Member. Promotion leaves the ceiling frozen; downgrade tightens immediately; below Maintain removes Leader. Raising/restoring authority needs separately approved governance, never the old grant alone.
- Direct human revocation stops affected writes immediately. Preserve grant history and persist status through approved governance; changing role/task allocation does not revoke account credentials or terminate another process. Same-actor new sessions may retain a verified role, never inherit task claims.
- Bootstrap/Recovery is explicit, repository/source/account/actor/proposal/action-bound **current-session** authority, not persistent Leader. Interruption or binding drift requires fresh confirmation. Use the caller's eligibility/activation procedure and existing rules; proposed files cannot authorize themselves. Persistent elevation occurs only after approved merge and same-SHA config/authorization read-back.
- Unknown applicable rules are not absent. Report verified unsupported enforcement without weakening stronger gates. Refresh current default authority/account/protection/scope at formal review, reassignment, merge or governance boundaries. Shared-account statements prove neither independent human consent, native other-account approval nor old-process termination.
